# Release L — 2.12.0, idea-to-design's optional deep dive (2026-10-19)

## What changed

- **idea-to-design step 9, "Deep dive (optional)".** After a clean consistency pass, while areas
  still have open items, the skill offers to go deeper before any hand-off. The offer lists those
  areas, proposes an order, and recommends. Each chosen area is then worked through in turn:
  its open items one question at a time, with large content lists proposed as drafts; one check
  of its rules now that the details exist; a consistency check of the area; and it is marked
  **refined**. The person can stop after any area and resume later. The hand-off moves to step 10.
- **Conduct:** the recommendation comes before the question, and nothing follows the question.
- **references/questions.md §9:** proposing a long content list as an editable draft.
- **Vision README template:** the still-to-design table gains a Status column (open / refined).
- **Evals:** two new scenarios, deep-dive-offer and deep-dive-area. The handoff scenario's
  prompt now declines the deep dive, so that it keeps testing the hand-off itself.

## Results

All eval agents ran on the same mid-tier model. "Before" is the 2.11.0 skill; "after" is this
release.

| Scenario | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| deep-dive-offer | 0/3 runs offer a deep dive; they go straight to project-bootstrap (assertions 2/4, 3/4, 2/4) | 9/9 runs offer it; the strict "one question, as the last line" format holds in 5/9 (2/3 on the final wording) |
| deep-dive-area | 3/3 (non-target: when asked, the existing question method already handles an area) | 3/3 |
| handoff (deep dive declined) | — | 3/3 |

The deep-dive-offer runs that miss the strict format still make the offer. They either put the
recommendation after the question, or phrase the question as a statement ("The question is
whether…"). Two wording changes reduced this: recommendation as its own part before the
question, and "nothing follows the question" in Conduct. The remaining variance is accepted.

`python -m pytest tests -q` passes; `check_docs.py --root .` reports 0 errors, 0 warnings.

## Known limits

- Only the offer and an area's first question are tested. The rest of an area (the draft
  content list, the rules check, the refined mark, the resume after stopping) has no scenario.
